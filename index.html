Cyber Snake malware is an advanced malicious software that secretly steals sensitive data such as passwords, banking details and personal files. It spreads via phishing emails, fake downloads and infected links. Once active, it runs silently and gives attackers remote access to the system.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a keylogger?
A keylogger is malicious software that records every keystroke you make on your device. It can capture passwords, credit card numbers, personal messages, and other sensitive information without your knowledge.
How does your detection system work?
Our AI-powered system analyzes typing patterns, keystroke timing, and behavioral anomalies. Machine learning algorithms detect deviations from normal patterns that indicate keylogging attempts, providing real-time protection.
Will it slow down my computer?
No! Our lightweight detection engine uses minimal system resources. Most users report zero noticeable performance impact. The system runs silently in the background without affecting your work.
Is my data private and secure?
